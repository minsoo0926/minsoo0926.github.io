title: Humor and Taste
date: Apr 14, 2026
---

When life gets hard, we usually give up laughter first. But whenever life
feels tough and everything seems to be going wrong, you might think laughter
is not a direct solution to your problem — yet, almost always, laughter can be
the solution to **your life** itself. I want to share my thoughts on
**humor and taste**, two of the most important things in my life, in the hope
that they may help you find happiness and a sense of self-fulfillment.

**Taste** is an essential ingredient of happiness. It is not just about food,
but about everything we like and dislike in daily life — music, art, fashion,
even people. There is no good or bad taste; what matters is finding your own
and enjoying it. I love rap and rock, especially *Kanye West*'s music
(my favourite album is *Ye*), and recently I have been listening to a lot of
80s soul. I like funny and violent movies, and I like to watch *Tarantino*
and *Kubrick*. I like bouldering, and running empty circuits when every
light is off. And I like people who share their favourites with me. These
things make me feel alive, happy, and like myself.

**Humor** is what makes life more enjoyable. Meeting friends and people you
love, sharing jokes and silly stories, and laughing together — these can keep
you from frustration and stress. Whenever a tough situation comes, I try to
laugh out loud and find something funny in it. After that, some solution can
usually be found, and once everything is over, I share the story with friends
over a cigarette and more laughter. Being frustrated and doing nothing only
traps you in a vicious circle; the only way out is to find some small,
trivial, funny thing inside it and enjoy that.

When taste meets humor, the combination becomes the best thing for your life.
It can make you more creative and open-minded, and at times it becomes a
shelter when you are going through hard times. So try to find your own taste
and humor, share them with your friends, and enjoy your life. That will be
the best way to find happiness and self-fulfillment.
